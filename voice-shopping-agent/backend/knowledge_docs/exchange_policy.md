# Exchange Policy

- Exchanges for a different size or color are allowed within 10 days of delivery.
- The item must be unworn and in its original condition to qualify.
- Only one exchange is permitted per order item.
- Exchanges are subject to stock availability; a refund is offered if unavailable.
- No additional fee applies for size or color exchanges within Pakistan.
- Exchange requests can be started from order history or by contacting support.
- Processing an exchange takes 3-5 business days after the original item is received.
