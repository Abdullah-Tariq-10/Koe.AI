# Shipping Policy

- Standard delivery within Karachi takes 2-3 business days.
- Delivery to other cities across Pakistan takes 4-6 business days.
- Orders above PKR 5,000 qualify for free standard shipping.
- Orders below PKR 5,000 are charged a flat PKR 200 shipping fee.
- Express delivery is available for an extra PKR 500, arriving within 24-48 hours in major cities.
- Tracking links are sent via email and SMS once an order ships.
- We do not currently ship internationally.
