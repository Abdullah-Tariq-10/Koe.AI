# Payment Information

- We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), debit/credit cards, and bank transfers via JazzCash/Easypaisa.
- COD orders are capped at PKR 15,000; higher orders require online prepayment.
- Card details are never stored on our servers.
- A COD convenience fee of PKR 100 applies to orders under PKR 2,000.
- Bank transfer confirmations may take up to 1 business day to reflect.
- Refunds for prepaid orders go back to the original payment method only.
